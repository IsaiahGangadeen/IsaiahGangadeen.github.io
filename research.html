Selected papers and projects

Under review / working paper

Oil Extraction under Production-Sharing Agreements: Constraints, Development Timing, and Evidence from Guyana

Develops a multi-field oil-extraction model with contractual, physical, and policy constraints to study output, investment timing, and rent distribution.

Methods: dynamic optimization, simulation, stochastic oil prices, policy counterfactuals.

Working paper

Resource Curse and Emissions Pathways: An Emissions Decomposition of the Effects of Oil Production on Tropical Oil-Producing Countries

Estimates the effects of oil-production onset on sectoral structure, energy intensity, emissions intensity, and population using a common-weight synthetic control framework.

Methods: structured synthetic control, ridge augmentation, conformal inference.

Research project

Converting Innovation Resources to Innovation Outcomes: Innovation Efficiency in Caribbean Firms

Examines how efficiently Caribbean firms convert innovation resources into product, process, organizational, and marketing innovation outcomes.

Methods: output-oriented VRS DEA, bootstrap truncated regression, firm-level survey data.

Research project

Rethinking Oil-led Development in Guyana: Structural Transformation and its Economic and Environmental Outcomes

Studies the channels through which resource-led growth shapes structural transformation and long-run economic and environmental outcomes.

Dissertation

Oil Wealth in a Carbon Sink: Rethinking Development and Environmental Compatibility

The dissertation examines whether oil-led development in forest-rich economies can be reconciled with climate objectives. Its central argument is that compatibility is neither automatic nor impossible: outcomes depend on economic structure, institutions, fiscal choices, contractual design, and preservation of natural carbon sinks.
