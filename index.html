Isaiah Gangadeen, PhD

Applied Economist and Lecturer in the Department of Economics at the University of Guyana, specializing in the microeconomic foundations of innovation, technology adoption, and development transitions.

My research interests include industrial organization, energy and resource economics, environmental economics, and development. I use quantitative and computational methods including causal inference, optimization, economic modelling, and policy simulation.
